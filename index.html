ABOUT OUR CENTRE

GEPCO Training & Development Centre

GEPCO Training and Development Centre, Nandipur, Gujranwala is a professional training facility providing structured learning and development opportunities for GEPCO employees.

The centre provides technical, commercial, clerical, computer and other professional training courses through qualified faculty and well-equipped training facilities.
